About Us

Built From One Vision

Founded in 2014 by Mahlogonolo Dinake, known as HyperSOUL-X, Hyper Production (SA) grew from a clear idea: independent music deserves thoughtful presentation, strong technical foundations, and a label that respects the artist behind the release.

The label is independent in more than name. Its direction comes from the person who built it, shaped by a hands-on approach to music, design, technology, and the everyday work of keeping a label moving.

One Person Behind Every Detail

HyperSOUL-X personally manages the day-to-day operation of Hyper Production (SA), including its social media, website, and technical infrastructure. The label’s visual identity is hands-on too: the logo, release artwork, and typography are all part of the creative work behind the brand.

From creative decisions to practical problem-solving, the work is handled with direct involvement at every stage. That means the label’s sound, presentation, and online presence are connected by one consistent vision—not passed between layers of a larger operation.

We Sign Music, Not Artists

Hyper Production (SA) works release by release. Artists keep their name, identity, and freedom to pursue other opportunities; the label focuses on the release itself and the work needed to present it professionally.

From artwork and promotion to distribution and the technical infrastructure behind a release, the aim is to give each project a clear identity and a solid route to listeners. Independent music deserves care without unnecessary restrictions—and that principle remains at the heart of the label.
